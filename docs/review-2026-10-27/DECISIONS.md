# Product quality review · 27 October 2026

## Review sequence

1. Inventory the actual shipped registry and source ownership, not superseded templates.
2. Review each screen for first use and repeated use, including relevant empty, pending, error and completed states.
3. Challenge proposed changes against the final mounted application and prior product decisions.
4. Inspect the actual rendered pages in all three editions, including supporting content below the fold.
5. Implement confirmed issues, then repeat functional, accessibility and visual checks before publication.

An award is an ambition, not a verification result. The release bar is observable clarity, accurate state, recoverable tasks, consistent controls, readable text and complete tested journeys.

## Product decisions to preserve

- Everyday, Minna and Hibiscus retain their own identity, typefaces, accents and isolated data.
- Retain the restored Hibiscus Home illustration. The 25 October request meant that its illustration was missing; the subsequent implementation restored it. Preserve the one-illustration-per-screen direction.
- Home stays focused; the main menu remains Plan. Header QR uses Scan/My code; the Chat icon remains familiar.
- Savings hack actions remain with Fund and Transfer back. General education sits lower, with first/returning guidance behavior.
- Existing financial calculations, ledger boundaries, review, PIN, consent, partner approval, mandates and member roles remain authoritative.
- Keep the collection journey order while improving truthful wording and unavailable-action recovery.

## Rejected or deferred directions

- Do not redesign an already-correct Plan CTA based on an overridden source template.
- Do not remove the current Hibiscus Home illustration or replace the established typefaces.
- Do not darken all separators or change muted text that already meets its purpose.
- Do not blanket-hide subtitles: some contain recipients, source accounts, warnings or required instructions.
- Do not reorder collection mandates/member approval as part of presentation polish.
- Do not cache PINs, skip legal/identity steps, or continue a payment automatically after sign-in.

Domain audit documents and the route inventory retain detailed evidence. Implementation remains gated on the visual review and bounded file ownership.
