# Lumo Monochrome review · 27 October 2026

## Final direction

Original, minimalist and mature Lumo interface. Black, white and pure greys form the base; acid lime #d7ff3f is the sole colour. The brief explicitly released prior typography, illustration and layout constraints while preserving the agreed product capabilities. This changes the existing Minna edition only.

The system uses local Manrope, open spacing, restrained rounded surfaces and high-contrast amounts. The original wordmarks remain exact. Legacy campus cartoons are suppressed throughout this edition; the welcome has a new, code-native aperture motif. The phone layout remains the product; desktop frames the same app.

## What changed

- New Home hierarchy: current money left after Budget (or actual available money before a Budget exists), date/cost basis, actual available balance, three money actions and separate savings.
- Stable amount privacy, including a neutral CSS-only savings sizing reservation. No actual private value is copied into hidden sizing text or attributes.
- Unified buttons, forms, notices, root headers, task sheets, navigation, focus, spacing and status presentation. Control edges use #888888.
- Budget, Money Boxes, saving rules and collection roles/mandates have coherent neutral surfaces and clearer numerical hierarchy.
- Payments, requests, reviews, receipts, QR and Cards share consistent amount entry and status styling. QR symbols remain pure black/white.
- Pause uses an ink surface with selective lime projected consequences. Optional alternatives live under Other ways forward. Main decisions and immediate cancellation remain outside the disclosure.
- Chat, Money Story, You, account details, support and settings use the same typography and surface rules.

## Preserved authority

Shared financial scripts are byte-for-byte pinned to the approved baseline. Account namespace, authentication, approval, consent, pending-payment recovery, separate operational/savings balances, savings-hack limits/idempotency, collection mandates, QR decoding and browser history remain under their original controllers. Fifteen new actual-index tests protect the new Home and Welcome presentation, plus Pause disclosure reveal, quotes, reading minimum, hold, separate PIN and cancellation.

## Boundaries

This remains an interactive sample-money prototype. No live bank, provider, identity, biometric or real transfer security is claimed. Camera capture is stubbed in layout fixtures. Browser fixtures exercise prepared first/returning states; the native history/financial regressions preserve existing task behaviour. No physical-device compatibility or award claim is made.
