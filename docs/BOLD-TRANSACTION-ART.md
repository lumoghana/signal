# Bold transaction illustration pass

These five production scene assets were generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool on 18 October 2026. Each scene used its own call, rather than a shared atlas. The approved Home master was inspected first and supplied as the male identity and style reference. The confirmed scene also used the inspected Welcome composition to preserve the recurring female cast.

The final PNGs were copied unchanged from the generated outputs into `dist/assets/clarity-bold/`. Original alpha, colour and raster dimensions are retained. All are 1536 × 1024 RGBA with true transparent pixels. No raster editing, recolouring, redrawing or post-generation cleanup was applied.

## Scene contract

| Asset | Meaning | Acting and restriction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `bold-payment.png` | Start or prepare a payment | Forward lunge and phone offered toward the viewer; no cash handover. |
| `bold-pause.png` | A proposed consequence comes into focus | Two open palms compare possibilities, thoughtful expression; no anxiety or unrelated purchase props. |
| `bold-plan.png` | Make room for what matters | Low active crouch, book and backpack; no ambiguous graph or embedded amounts. |
| `bold-funding.png` | Funding or money received | Arms open, phone lifted, relief and possibility; no person handing over money. |
| `bold-confirmed.png` | Confirmed success only | Two recurring students in a full-body high-five, separate from pending and failure states. |

These scenes deliberately contain no ring. The interface owns the insight ring around actual calculated conclusions. Monochrome Legon context is supplied as a separate page layer, allowing the scene and live financial text to remain readable. These PNGs contain no financial text, amounts, UI or status claims.
