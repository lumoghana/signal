## Verification limits

The supported browser-control capability is unavailable. Following the Sites workflow, no preview server, alternate browser or screenshot automation was started. Actual Safari/Chrome rendering, physical keyboard placement, enlarged-text layouts, touch-drag arbitration and screenshots/recording remain unverified. The sheet has explicit Back and Close actions and never depends on dragging. Source/DOM checks do not establish physical-device fit.

The art is flat transparent PNG artwork, not rigged character animation. Shared reveal/hold/resolve motion animates the composition and sheet stages; it does not animate hands or financial values independently. Long content can scroll naturally. Original projects remain separate and no personal records were imported.
