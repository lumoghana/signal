# Lumo · Clarity Edition system

**The consequence comes into focus.** The activity and its recognisable prop determine the illustration. Lumo's established lime accent identifies useful financial intelligence. The interface does not scatter decorative rings around the experience.

| Rule | Implementation |

| Amounts | Shared `L.money`: GH₵600.00, GH₵1,234.56, −GH₵30.00. Two decimals, true minus and tabular numerals. Entry keeps GH₵ together. Figures remain real text and never animate as counters. |
| Wordmark | Unchanged Black and White capital-L SVGs, native 2502:712 ratio. No text recreation. The actual wordmark is preserved; a purposeful illustration may echo its o geometry. |

Lime is a text-width highlight for Home's left-after-plan figure, the projected Pause comparison value, the latest account-backed financial Chat conclusion and live entry consequence. The active main-navigation item also uses lime with a short underline, as an explicit location cue. It does not colour every heading, control, graph or piece of clothing. Meaning remains explicit in words and numbers. |

Home preserves the primary **Left after your plan** figure and smaller, separately labelled available account balance. Those two summaries are visible on a fresh unlocked load, successful sign-in and completed sign-up. A visible eye-and-text control says “Hide balances” or “Show balances” and toggles both without moving money or rebuilding Home. Manual hiding lasts through navigation; backgrounding, page departure, back-forward cache restoration and locking conceal the amounts. Visibility remains session-only. Allocations and payment details stay visible. Plan dates remain in My plan. Account breakdown opens from the plan explanation sheet. Home omits the date/change line, privacy instruction and separate Balance details link. Home has three groups: money now, receiving actions and Money Story. The main amount stays 44px/600; available balance stays 25px/400. One money group has no internal divider. Money Story uses a 13px caption including the seven-day period and one 16px/500 sentence containing the category amount and total, with accurate wording for transfers, ties and single-category histories. No separate support line, bar or category grid appears on Home. Full categories, other periods and recent payments open in a detail sheet; long history remains a dedicated screen. Add money and Request money are visible directly as 54px outlined utilities with 16px labels, Home-only hover/focus styles and no visible decorative icons. Spending actions and saved-payment continuation live in Pay. Important plan gaps stay visible.

Short actions continue in one native-style sheet above Home, Pay, Plan, Chat or You. Payments, funding, planning, records, account controls and help reuse the host without moving the parent page. Onboarding, recovery, long Activity/story reading and the merchant console retain full-page navigation and the same visual rules. The host now supplies six semantic treatments, natural content sizing and resize diagnostics. `native-refinement.css` loads last. See [native layout rules](NATIVE-REFINEMENT-SYSTEM.md).

Entry has one compact live consequence. Review owns recipient, amount, fee and total. A qualifying Pause owns the consequence, one projected comparison and accessible alternatives. Pause arithmetic follows the decision actions in an optional disclosure; Chat arithmetic opens a saved-answer detail sheet. Existing confirmed-success logic alone changes actual money. Preview art and interface wording never imply a completed transaction.

Activity mapping uses the actual route before old illustration aliases. My plan cannot inherit a laptop just because an older template requested `goals`; More uses its own portrait/details card rather than inheriting an identity-verification action from the alias `identity`; Review remains a payment check even when its old alias is `pay`. A laptop appears only for a selected laptop goal or a supported laptop-only goal trade-off. Food-counter art requires a selected Campus Pay food merchant. Food handover additionally requires explicit delivered status and a confirmed payment. Generic and mixed goals use the notebook; pending, failed, reversed and unknown artwork requests stay neutral. Compact payment results use one factual status symbol and no character scene.

Chat begins with **Ask your money**, an engaged student and starter questions. The conversation takes over after the first question. Only the latest financial insight receives lime; ordinary help responses and old answers remain in ink. History and working next actions retain the authoritative engine. The conversation scrolls independently; bounded optional tools and a workspace overflow fallback preserve access with enlarged text. Calculations and quick edits return to the same conversation and unsent input.

Goals remain planning commitments, not additional account debits. Expected allowance remains unreceived money. No new warning thresholds or date forecasts were added. All banking, identity checks and payments remain simulated.

First-plan setup has two core steps: **Costs to cover → Review**. Its introduction defines a plan and explains how Lumo uses it; an optional labelled, isolated example demonstrates the consequence. Cost suggestions fill names only; the student enters a total, with a live shared-engine preview. Review captures the final unsent cost. Goals, expected income and dates are optional disclosures or related sheet states. Expected income is excluded from available money; a date does not multiply costs. Save is atomic, moves no money and returns to Home. Dismissal and navigation retain unfinished fields and the current step. My plan lists essentials before optional goals and expected money. Incoming, pending, failed, reversed, future and invalid payments are excluded. Goal allocations are planning commitments and do not count as payments. Home uses a rolling **Last 7 days** window.
