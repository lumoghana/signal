### Make the known inputs clear

The early sample now states its starting facts before the consequence: GH₵600 available, GH₵350 for food and transport, and GH₵100 set aside for a laptop. These figures come from the isolated engine fixture, not from personal account data. The sample still creates no plan or payment.

A one-cost plan therefore says Based on your 1 saved cost. Goals alone explicitly say no costs saved. Paid and pending-linked costs remain saved inputs; unpaid calculations remain the engine’s responsibility. Expected income and unallocated targets do not inflate the count. First-plan Review says draft rather than saved. This is a statement of known inputs, not an invented completeness score. Other costs are not included.

The period selector omits the unavailable expected-income option until a valid future date exists. A previously selected stale income period remains visible for correction and retains the existing validation. No date or forecast is invented.

### One plain vocabulary

New Pause and Chat conclusions say Money set aside for Laptop/goals. Detail rows use Money set aside for goals and Goal money uncovered. ### A verified draft adjustment

`plan-adjustment.js` offers one smaller amount on a qualifying plan shortfall when the current payment is editable and a positive saved cost exists. With the standard GH₵600/350/100 setup, a GH₵280 extra purchase can offer Use GH₵150 · Keeps your saved plan covered. Fixed requests, merchant-priced sales and bill products are excluded. No suggestion is made when the existing gap cannot be removed by changing the payment.

The helper searches valid pesewa amounts using the existing plan-impact engine and authoritative fee function, then verifies the result. Linked-cost fulfilment and pending holds therefore receive the same treatment as payment entry and Chat. For a fee-bearing transfer, the display distinguishes the payment amount from its checked total. The claim concerns saved commitments only; it is not a guarantee about unentered costs.

The action appears with the revealed consequence. It is an edit: it preserves the recipient, note and plan link, invalidates the previous review/acceptance and returns to payment Review. It never opens PIN directly or moves money. A rendered quote is bound to its draft, amount, fee and current engine signature. If the funds or plan have changed, the action returns to Review without silently applying a new quote. A stale detached control cannot change another payment. Existing Edit amount, Continue, link, defer and cancel remain available.
