# Contextual illustration correction

This pass restores the original Lumo illustration identity and gives each drawing a direct product context. The original `student-expressive.png`, `thoughtful-refined-4.13.png` and `friends-editorial.png` were inspected before generation. They remain unchanged.

The original curly-haired male student is the authority: dot eyes, angular long nose, black line work, white filled face and limbs, young adult proportions, ochre/orange/navy/cream Kente shirt and white trousers. Both new scenes preserve this casual apparel rather than the later black blazer and green-cuff interpretation.

| `/assets/clarity-context/chat-question.png` | Same student asks a question on his upright phone, curious eyebrow, open mouth and conversational free hand | New Home scene plus original thoughtful scene |

No green, black blazer, physical hoop, arbitrary held shapes, financial values, icons or interface text appear in either scene. Campus architecture is intentionally absent so the existing monochrome Legon background can sit behind each scene as a separate layer. Money and calculated conclusions remain real interface elements.

## Production and checks

The built-in image-generation tool produced one separate asset per call, with `transparent_background: true`. Final outputs were inspected and copied without pixel editing into `dist/assets/clarity-context/`. No original illustration was overwritten. Both files are 1536 × 1024 RGBA PNGs with transparent corners. A pixel check found no green-dominant visible pixels. These are complete flat image masters, not independently rigged body parts.

Visual self-review: Home is recognisably the original Lumo character, with a real Kente shirt, exactly two arms, an upright phone and grounded seated posture. Chat uses the same garment, face and silhouette, while its open-mouth expression and raised eyebrow communicate asking rather than generic consideration. The Home pose deliberately stays quiet; expressiveness comes from the hand and face rather than acrobatics. The two scenes are intentionally related and need to be sized differently in the interface so the full-body Chat drawing does not dominate an active conversation. At small sizes the fine shirt motifs will simplify perceptually, while the main orange/navy woven panels remain readable. Final perceived scale and compositing still require rendered-device review.
