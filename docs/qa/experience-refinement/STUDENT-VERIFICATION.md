# Unassisted student verification

Use disposable sample accounts in each prototype. These tasks are a protocol for the next observed test, not evidence that students have completed them. Do not enter real identity information, send real money or message a real accountability partner.

Recruit ten students who have not been coached through Lumo. Let each choose the manual or Chat path. The observer records what happens without explaining where to tap. Ask about decisions after the task, rather than interrupting the flow.

1. Create a Budget from “I have GH₵600 to last until Friday. I don’t know what to budget.” Let the student supply their own assumed costs. Record whether they can explain that a Budget does not move or lock money.
2. Change Food in Chat, review it, return to Edit costs and change it again. Record task completion, assistance, taps and lost or duplicated information.
3. Ask whether lunch for GH₵80 fits. Record whether the student can choose planned Food or extra spending and explain why those consequences differ.
4. Make a simulated discretionary payment that triggers the Pause. Record whether the student reads, changes the amount, continues or cancels. Immediately ask: “What would this payment change?” and “Has any money moved yet?” The existing900ms reveal,3-second reading window and90ms confirmation press are retained; do not infer comprehension from waiting for the button.
5. Open “Help me understand this” from that Pause, ask a question, then return to the same payment. Record whether amount, merchant and source remain clear and whether authentication is still required.
6. Complete a simulated payment. Ask the student to find the confirmed Budget effect and distinguish it from current account money. Include a pending-result task and check that the student does not mistake it for completed payment.
7. Open an ended Budget review. Ask what Lumo knows, what the unknown spending figure means, and whether the comparison includes spending outside Lumo. Try an older Budget with no reliable start date.
8. Keep the same amounts or adjust the next Budget. Record whether the student understands the review is a proposal until explicitly saved and that no money moves.

For each task record completion without help, critical errors, taps, scrolls, time, first point of confusion and a short quote. Separate errors caused by prototype limitations from wording or navigation problems. Test ordinary phone text size first, then larger text and reduced motion. Observe real iOS/Android keyboards when devices are available.

The main acceptance evidence is successful task completion and an accurate explanation of the financial consequence. Automated tests verify implementation rules; this observed test measures whether students understand and can use those rules.
