# Shared flows from Hibiscus v10

Source: published Hibiscus v10, commit 3b50b300896ccb2b67d1044bc6840fbcd1daff1e.

Everyday and Minna retain their own identity, storage namespace, colours, fonts and original edition-specific Home with privacy inside the main amount card, four payment/funding actions and a separate Money Boxes row. Their five navigation destinations now follow Hibiscus: Money Story, Plan, Home, Cards and Pay. You remains available in each root header and Ask Lumo opens full-screen Chat with return to its origin.

Shared additions include funded virtual card creation and its separate authorisation; top-ups, holds, freeze, limits, receipts and refunds; Money Story charts, merchant/category drilldowns and scoped Chat; Budget progress and collapsed items; balance calculations and first-Budget/Chat clarity. The card engines reserve and debit the correct account, exclude internal funding from spending, and retain the existing Pause policy. No customer records are copied between editions.

The getting-started page uses Hibiscus’s welcoming composition in green for Everyday and monochrome for Minna. Home retains its earlier illustrated layout with Add money, Request money, Campus Pay, Transfers and Money Boxes. Money Story and Virtual Cards remain in their own tabs. New root screens own their visual content so automatic campus illustrations cannot displace their headers. Navigation preserves retained parent nodes when sheets close. The new feature stylesheet inherits each edition's presentation.

Successful sign-in always opens Home with the balance eye closed. Stored return routes are cleared, all Home amounts stay hidden until the eye is tapped, and unfinished payments remain drafts.

These are browser-local simulations. Native hardware and live banking/issuer integrations are outside this update.
